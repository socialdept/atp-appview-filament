# Labelers

Part of the [atp-appview-filament docs](../introduction.md#documentation). The labelers the index follows, and what each of their labels does here.

The labelers the index follows, through Tack or their own streams, with each one's profile, connection, labels kept, and labels received from Tack or the last label from its stream. Follow a labeler by handle or DID, and unfollow one, which drops its labels along with anything they hide or flag.

A labeler's page lists the values it declares with their names, severity, what they blur and the labeler's default, what each does here on content and on accounts, and how many are in force, with a Change action to set a policy or return to the default.

The page shows only when atp-appview's moderation feature is on. Following works when `atp-appview.labels.source` is `streams` or `atp-appview.labels.tack.url` points at a Tack.
